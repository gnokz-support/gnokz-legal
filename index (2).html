Version 2026-10-06 · By creating an artist in the app you accept these terms.

  1. You own the music you upload, or you have the rights to release it.
  2. No copyrighted songs, samples or parts of other people's music without permission.
  3. AI tools are allowed only if the vocals are your own real voice and the beat is modified from your own original beat. No AI voices or voice clones.
  4. Artwork must be clean: no logos, brand marks or watermarks.
  5. Songs can be up to 5 minutes long.
  6. Every artist and release is reviewed by the Gnokz team before it appears on Gnokz Music.
  7. Gnokz can reject, hide or remove content that breaks these rules. Repeated serious violations can suspend the artist account.
  8. Do not pretend to be another artist. Impersonation is removed.
  9. You must be 18 or older to release music with Gnokz for Artists.
  10. You keep the rights to your music. You give GNOKZ Production permission to store, stream and show your approved music, artwork, name and photos on Gnokz Music, for free and worldwide, until it is removed.
  11. If a release is not approved, you can appeal once within 30 days of the decision.
  12. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Account → Delete my account). An artist you created and own alone is then removed from Gnokz Music with its songs.
  13. Questions: gnokz.support@gmail.com.
