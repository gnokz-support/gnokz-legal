This policy is for artists and their teams. It explains what Gnokz for Artists collects, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it. One Gnokz account works for both Gnokz Music and Gnokz for Artists.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, a display name and whether your email is verified. Your password is handled by a secure sign-in service; we never see it.
  • Your artist: artist name, genre, bio, profile photo and cover photo.
  • Your music: song files, artwork, song details (title, credits, lyrics, explicit), your AI-use answers and declarations, an optional original beat, proof files and appeal messages.
  • Credits: the names and roles of the people who made each song (producers, writers, engineers, musicians, label, publisher, cover art).
  • Lyrics Sync: the timed lines of your synced lyrics, the reaction delay you used, the "Lyrics synced by" name you type, and when your team agreed to the Lyrics of your music terms (which version, and which account agreed).
  • Your artist page: concerts, events and livestreams, merch (with its photo and store link), official links, your booking email, your Artist Pick (its photo and note) and the HOOKs you choose with their captions.
  • Your team: the emails and roles of the people you add, and a security log of important actions in the workspace (who did what, and when).
  • Claims: the links or documents you send to prove you are an artist, and one-time email codes (stored only as a secure code, never in plain text).
  • Messages: review results in your Inbox, and the lyrics or explicit changes you send for review with your note.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a device code so we can send Inbox decisions to your phone.
  • Listening numbers: plays and HOOK plays on Gnokz Music are counted with coded IDs (never a listener's name or email), only to show you totals.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID or other apps' data. The app only opens the photos and files you pick. Audio you pick from your phone for Lyrics Sync is played on your phone and is not uploaded.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and your artist workspace, and to let your team work together with the access you give them.
  • To review every artist and release before it appears, and to publish approved music on Gnokz Music.
  • To show your lyrics, synced lyrics, credits and artist page to listeners.
  • To show you statistics for your music (counted with coded IDs, never listeners' names or emails).
  • To send your Inbox decisions to your phone, and to tell your followers about a new single, HOOK or show (at most one update per artist per day).
  • To keep Gnokz safe: stop spam, copied music, fake voices, impersonation and account misuse, and act on copyright notices.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who can see it

  • Everyone on Gnokz Music, after approval: your artist name, genre, bio, photos, approved songs with artwork, title, credits and lyrics, synced lyrics with the "Lyrics synced by" name, your HOOKs and captions, Artist Pick, shows, merch, official links and booking email.
  • Your followers: a short update with the title of your new single, HOOK or show.
  • Your team: the artist workspace (singles, statistics, Inbox, credits, lyrics) according to the role the Owner gives each member.
  • The Gnokz review team: what you send for review, including private files, proof and appeals. Decisions reach you from "the Gnokz team"; reviewers' names and details are kept private.
  • Reports: if a listener reports your song or page, you are told the reason, never who reported it.
  • Trusted service providers that host sign-in, notifications, servers and file storage for us. They may only use your data to run Gnokz and must keep it confidential.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Private files of a rejected or withdrawn release: deleted 30 days after the decision.
  • Proof files, original beats and review copies of an approved release: deleted 30 days after the decision.
  • Approved music, lyrics, synced lyrics, credits and artist details: kept while they are on Gnokz Music, or until you remove them.
  • Your account data: kept until you delete your account.
  • Your Lyrics terms agreement: kept while your account exists, as a record of what you agreed to.
  • Security logs: kept to protect the service; they contain an internal account number, not your files.
  • Notification device codes: until you sign out, delete your account, or the code stops working.
  • Reports about content: deleted 180 days after they are closed. Decisions on reports (hide, takedown, strikes, paused uploads) are kept to protect the service.
  • Listening counts: kept to show artists their totals; they use coded IDs, never names or emails.

Delete your account

  • In the app: tap the badge at the top right → Account → DELETE MY ACCOUNT. Enter your password and type DELETE.
  • Deleting removes your Gnokz account (also for Gnokz Music), your applications, unpublished singles and their files, your Inbox, change requests, song-detail requests, claims, team access, your Lyrics terms agreements, the reports you sent, notification device codes, playlists, follows, mutes, likes, comments and HOOK plays.
  • An artist that you created in Gnokz for Artists and own alone is removed from Gnokz Music with all its songs, lyrics, synced lyrics and credits. Artists that other Owners share, and existing Gnokz Music artists you claimed, stay; only your access ends, and their live songs stay without your name.
  • No app? Email gnokz.support@gmail.com from your account email and we delete it within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • You can see and change your artist details, lyrics, synced lyrics, credits and artist page in the app at any time.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or its deletion, and you can object to how we use it. Email gnokz.support@gmail.com.
  • Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Gnokz for Artists is for people 15 and older. If you are 15–17, read this policy with your parent or legal guardian; they can contact us about your data at any time.

Security

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Every request is checked against your signed-in account and your role in the workspace.
  • Files waiting for review are stored privately and are never shown on Gnokz Music.
  • Content that is hidden while a report is checked is never shown, announced or sent as a notification. Your team sees the reason in the app.
  • Only authorised Gnokz staff can review, each with their own account. Nobody reviews their own music.

Changes and contact

  • When this policy changes, the new version and date appear in the app and on the web page.
  • Questions or requests: gnokz.support@gmail.com.
