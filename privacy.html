This policy explains what Gnokz for Artists collects, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and how to delete it. Gnokz for Artists is run by GNOKZ Production (Reymark Delagao), Philippines. One Gnokz account works for both Gnokz Music and Gnokz for Artists.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, a display name and whether your email is verified. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it.
  • Artist details: artist name, genre, bio, profile photo and cover photo.
  • Music you send: song files, artwork, song details (title, credits, lyrics, explicit), your AI-use answers and declarations, an optional original beat, proof files and appeal messages.
  • Team and claims: the emails of people you add to your team, links or documents you send to claim an artist, and one-time email codes (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Messages and history: review results in your Inbox, and a security log of important actions (who did what, when).
  • Reports in Gnokz Music: when you report a song or an artist page, we store your account, the reason you picked and your note.
  • Review team: if you join the Gnokz review team, your name, email, role and the decisions and notes you make.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID or other apps' data. The app only opens the photos and files you pick.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and artist workspace, and to let your team work together.
  • To review every artist and release, and to publish approved music on Gnokz Music.
  • To show you listener statistics for your music (counted without identifying listeners).
  • To keep the service safe: stop spam, copied music, fake voices and account misuse.
  • To check reports: the review team can hide a song or artist page while checking, or take a song down. A takedown counts as a strike, and new uploads of that artist can be paused.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who can see it

  • Public on Gnokz Music after approval: your artist name, genre, bio, profile and cover photos, and your approved songs with their artwork, title, credits and lyrics.
  • The Gnokz review team: everything you send for review, including private files, proof and appeals.
  • Your team members: the artist workspace (singles, statistics, messages) according to their role.
  • Reports: only the review team sees them, with the reporter's email partly hidden. The reported artist is told the reason, never who reported.
  • Service providers who store and process data for us: Google Firebase (sign-in) and Cloudflare (servers, database and file storage).
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Private files of a rejected or withdrawn release: deleted 30 days after the decision.
  • Proof files, original beats and review copies of an approved release: deleted 30 days after the decision.
  • Approved music and artist details: kept while they are on Gnokz Music.
  • Your account data: kept until you delete your account.
  • Security logs: kept to protect the service; they contain an internal account number, not your files.
  • Anonymous listening counts: kept, because they do not identify anyone.
  • Reports: deleted 180 days after they are closed. Decisions on reports (hide, takedown, strikes, paused uploads) are kept to protect the service.
  • Review team records: when a team member deletes their account, their name and email are removed; their past decisions stay without a name.

Delete your account

  • In the app: tap the badge at the top right → Account → DELETE MY ACCOUNT. Enter your password and type DELETE.
  • Deleting removes your Gnokz account (also for Gnokz Music), your applications, singles and their files, your Inbox, change requests, claims, team access, the reports you sent, playlists, follows, likes and comments.
  • An artist that you created in Gnokz for Artists and own alone is removed from Gnokz Music with all its songs. Artists that other Owners share, and existing Gnokz Music artists you claimed, stay; only your access ends.
  • No app? Email gnokz.support@gmail.com from your account email and we delete it within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • You can see and change your artist details in the app, and ask us for a copy or a correction of your data by email.
  • Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act you can object, ask for access, correction or deletion, and complain to the National Privacy Commission.
  • Gnokz for Artists is for people 18 and older.

Security

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Every request is checked against your signed-in account and your role.
  • Files waiting for review are stored privately and are never shown on Gnokz Music.
  • The review team signs in with their own Gnokz accounts and a role (Reviewer or Lead). Nobody decides on their own music, and only the Owner can delete an account.

Changes and contact

  • When this policy changes, the new version and date appear here and on the web page.
  • Questions or requests: gnokz.support@gmail.com.
