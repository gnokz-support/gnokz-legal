Gnokz for Artists and Gnokz Music use the same Gnokz account. Deleting it deletes it for both.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Gnokz for Artists and sign in.
  2. Tap the round badge at the top right → Account.
  3. Tap DELETE MY ACCOUNT. You see which artists and songs will be removed.
  4. Enter your password, type DELETE and tap DELETE MY ACCOUNT FOREVER. It happens right away.

Without the app

Email gnokz.support@gmail.com from the email address of your Gnokz account with the subject Delete my Gnokz account. We confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted, what is kept

  • In the app: tap the badge at the top right → Account → DELETE MY ACCOUNT. Enter your password and type DELETE.
  • Deleting removes your Gnokz account (also for Gnokz Music), your applications, singles and their files, your Inbox, change requests, claims, team access, the reports you sent, playlists, follows, likes and comments.
  • An artist that you created in Gnokz for Artists and own alone is removed from Gnokz Music with all its songs. Artists that other Owners share, and existing Gnokz Music artists you claimed, stay; only your access ends.
  • No app? Email gnokz.support@gmail.com from your account email and we delete it within 30 days.

Private files of past reviews are deleted automatically 30 days after the decision. Anonymous listening counts, which do not identify anyone, are kept.
